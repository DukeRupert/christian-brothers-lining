sections:
  - eyebrow: "The Technology"
    heading: "How CIPP Works"
    paragraphs:
      - "A flexible, resin-saturated liner is inserted into the existing pipe and inflated against the pipe wall. Once cured — using hot water, steam, or UV light depending on the liner system — it hardens into a seamless, structurally independent pipe within the host pipe."
      - "The result is a pipe-within-a-pipe with no joints, no seams, and no corrodible material. CIPP liners installed to ASTM F1216 specifications carry a 50-year design life. No excavation is required at any stage of the process."
  - eyebrow: "Cost Comparison"
    heading: "CIPP vs. Open-Cut Replacement"
    paragraphs:
      - "Traditional pipe replacement requires excavation, shoring, pipe removal, new pipe installation, backfill, compaction, and surface restoration. In urban environments, that means traffic control, utility coordination, and pavement reconstruction."
      - "CIPP eliminates all of it. No trench, no spoil, no surface restoration. For most municipal mainline applications, CIPP is 30–50% less expensive than open-cut on a per-foot basis — and it's completed in a fraction of the time."
  - eyebrow: "Applications"
    heading: "Where We Use CIPP"
    paragraphs:
      - "CIPP is appropriate for gravity sanitary sewers, stormwater mains, and pressure pipes in a range of diameters. We work in pipe from 6\" to 60\" diameter. Common applications include:"
    items:
      - "Gravity sewer mainlines with root intrusion, joint displacement, or corrosion"
      - "Stormwater infrastructure with structural deterioration"
      - "Force mains and pressure pipes requiring corrosion-resistant relining"
      - "Pipes under roads, driveways, and structures where excavation is impractical"

process_eyebrow: "Our Process"
process_title: "From Assessment to Sign-Off"
process:
  - title: "CCTV Inspection"
    description: "We begin every project with a NASSCO PACP-coded camera inspection. This documents existing pipe condition, identifies structural defects, measures pipe dimensions, and confirms CIPP suitability. You receive a full written report before any liner is ordered."
  - title: "Cleaning & Preparation"
    description: "High-pressure hydro jetting removes root intrusion, scale, grease, and debris from the pipe wall. The host pipe must be clean to specification for the liner to achieve a full, consistent bond. This step is not skipped."
  - title: "Liner Installation"
    description: "A resin-saturated felt or fiberglass liner is inverted or pulled into position within the host pipe. It conforms precisely to the pipe's interior geometry — including bends, offset joints, and diameter variations."
  - title: "Curing & Lateral Reinstatement"
    description: "The liner is cured in place using the appropriate method for the liner system and site conditions. Lateral connections are reinstated robotically from within the pipe — no excavation at connection points."
  - title: "Final Inspection & Documentation"
    description: "A post-installation CCTV inspection confirms full liner seating, proper lateral reinstatement, and absence of voids or wrinkles. Full documentation is provided for your project records and asset management system."
